HOW I GOT HERE

Learning security by building it.

I’m a Management Information Systems student at The University of Alabama focused on cybersecurity and security engineering.

Most of my hands-on work starts with a simple question: what actually happens under the hood? I use virtual labs to build segmented networks, collect logs, test controls, generate security events, troubleshoot what breaks, and validate what the tools can really detect.
